02 · Sticky CTA barRequired · automatic

Keeps the primary actions in reach after the header scrolls out of view. Solves the ‘CTAs not persistent’ problem.

Example content is realistic but placeholder (Danfoss Turbocor TTH/TGH). Specs and claims are not verified. Bracketed ‘Image’ boxes stand for photography.

Responsive behaviour

MobileFixed to bottom of viewport; two equal buttons, 44px+ tall
TabletSlim bar under the nav; name + two buttons
Desktop / WideSlim bar under the nav; name, phone, selector link, two buttons

Editor / design guidance

  • Appears when the Product header leaves the viewport; hides again near the Contact band to avoid duplicating it.
  • Not editor-placed. Buttons use the brand shape.

Source of truth: docs/block-library.md, docs/design-logic.md. R = required, O = optional.
