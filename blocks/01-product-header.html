01 · Product headerRequired · first

Replaces the title banner and image-only hero. One full-width background image, darkened and blurred, with the product story laid directly over it: what it is, who makes it, and what to do next.

Example content is realistic but placeholder (Danfoss Turbocor TTH/TGH). Specs and claims are not verified. Bracketed ‘Image’ boxes stand for photography.

Content fields

FieldReq.Notes
BreadcrumbautoGenerated from product category
Manufacturer badgeOLogo from Partners library
Product nameR≤ 60 chars; H1
Sub-headingR≤ 140 chars, one sentence
Intro paragraphRA sizeable paragraph, ~50–90 words. Replaces the facts strip
Hero background imageROne image, full block width, ≈21:9 crop; focal point selectable; alt text required
Primary CTARDefault: Contact us
Secondary CTAODefault: Request a CPD
Tertiary linkODefault: Product selector; or Download datasheet

Variants

  • Left-aligned (default) — Text sits directly on the image, which has a dark overlay and blur.
  • Centred — For images with a central subject or symmetrical crop.

Responsive behaviour

MobileSame treatment; text block full width with full-width CTAs; the band grows in height to fit
TabletText block ~60% width over the left
Desktop / WideFull-bleed image; text block ≤ 640px, vertically centred; CTAs side by side

Editor / design guidance

  • No panel sits between text and image: legibility comes from a fixed dark overlay (~60%) plus ~6px background blur, with white text. Because the overlay is fixed, any photo is safe and contrast is predictable.
  • Pick images with a recognisable product; blur and darkening reduce detail, so avoid images that rely on fine text or small features.
  • Headline numbers go in the paragraph and in Technical specifications; there is no label/value strip.
  • CTAs and the Product selector link are inside the first viewport at 1440×800.

Source of truth: docs/block-library.md, docs/design-logic.md. R = required, O = optional.

Danfoss Turbocor® TTH/TGH Compressors

Oil-free, magnetic-bearing centrifugal compressors for high-efficiency chillers and heat pumps.

Danfoss Turbocor® TTH/TGH compressors use magnetic-bearing technology to deliver oil-free, variable-speed compression for chillers and heat pumps. With no oil system to maintain and strong part-load efficiency, they suit data centres, healthcare, process cooling and commercial buildings where reliability and running costs matter.
