Danfoss Turbocor® TTH/TGH Compressors

Oil-free, magnetic-bearing centrifugal compressors for high-efficiency chillers and heat pumps.

Danfoss Turbocor® TTH/TGH compressors use magnetic-bearing technology to deliver oil-free, variable-speed compression for chillers and heat pumps. With no oil system to maintain and strong part-load efficiency, they suit data centres, healthcare, process cooling and commercial buildings where reliability and running costs matter. Klima-Therm supplies, specifies and supports the range across the UK.

How it works

Oil-free compression

Magnetic levitation, no oil

The compressor shaft floats on digitally controlled magnetic bearings, so there is no metal-to-metal contact and no lubrication system to maintain.

  • Digital control adjusts bearing position thousands of times a second
  • Variable speed gives high part-load efficiency
  • Fewer moving parts means lower lifetime cost

Part-load performance

Variable-speed efficiency

Output that follows the load

Most systems spend most of their operating hours below full load. An integrated variable-speed drive lets the compressor match its output to demand instead of cycling on and off.

  • Smooth capacity control across the operating range
  • Soft start limits inrush current
  • Strong seasonal efficiency at part load

Low noise and vibration

Quiet running

Suited to noise-sensitive sites

With no metal-to-metal contact in the bearings, vibration and sound levels are low. That suits plant rooms close to occupied spaces, hospitals and urban rooftops.

  • Low vibration eases structural and isolation requirements
  • Lower sound levels help with planning noise limits
  • Quiet at part load, where most running hours occur

11 · FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What does “oil-free” mean in practice?
The compressor runs on magnetic bearings, so it needs no lubricating oil. That means no oil management system, oil changes or oil carry-over into the circuit.
Which refrigerants can it use?
Refrigerant options depend on the model. Check the specifications table or ask our team to confirm for your project.
Can it be retrofitted to an existing chiller?
Often, yes, but it depends on the chiller design and duty. Speak to an engineer for a feasibility check.
What maintenance is required?
Far less than conventional compressors, as there is no oil service. Routine checks and diagnostics are still recommended.
Do you offer CPD on this technology?
Yes. Request a CPD session and we will tailor it to your team.
